# Refunds Returns and Exchanges for Sellers

## Seller policy responsibility

Each Etsy seller is responsible for their own policies regarding refunds, exchanges, and returns. Individual policies vary from shop to shop.

Sellers are required to set a return policy whenever they edit or create a new listing for a physical item, even if the policy tells buyers that returns or exchanges are not accepted.

## Return agreements and buyer communication

If a seller enters into a return agreement with a buyer through Messages or Etsy’s case system, Etsy requires the seller to fulfill that agreement. This may include:

- Issuing a refund for returned items.
- Providing Etsy with proof of shipping for a replacement item.

If there is a problem with a transaction, Etsy recommends contacting the buyer directly through Messages.

## Etsy Purchase Protection

If an order is eligible for Etsy Purchase Protection, Etsy will cover up to $250, or the converted equivalent in the seller’s local currency, of a refund. Any remaining amount is charged to the seller, and the seller does not need to issue the refund themselves.

Etsy Purchase Protection may apply when:

- A buyer never received the package, for example if it was lost in transit.
- The item arrived after its estimated delivery date window.
- The item arrived damaged, for the first instance per calendar year.
- The item matches the listing description and photos, but a buyer claims it does not.

For cases opened prior to May 7, 2026, the Etsy Purchase Protection policy in effect at that time applies.

Buyers are responsible for customs duties, import duties, and other government charges payable on delivery. Items that arrive late or are not received because the buyer delayed or refused to pay charges owed on delivery do not qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection. Sellers are not responsible for refunds in that situation, but may offer them at their discretion.

## European Union and United Kingdom

Transactions with buyers located in the European Union or United Kingdom are subject to local consumer protection regulations, such as the EU Directive on Consumer Rights.
