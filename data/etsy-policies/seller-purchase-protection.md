# Purchase Protection for Sellers

If an order is eligible for Etsy Purchase Protection, Etsy covers up to $250 of a refund, or the converted equivalent in the seller’s local currency. Any remaining amount is charged to the seller, and the seller does not need to issue the refund.

Etsy Purchase Protection may apply when:

- A buyer never received the package, for example if it was lost in transit.
- The item arrived 7+ days after its estimated delivery date window provided at checkout.
- The item arrived damaged, for the first instance per calendar year.
- The item matches the listing description and photos, but a buyer claims it does not.

For cases opened before May 7, 2026, the policy in effect at that time applies.

## What isn’t eligible?

An order is ineligible when:

- It does not meet one or more eligibility requirements.
- The item does not match the listing description.
- The seller has third-party insurance or carrier/payment processor coverage and does not first submit a claim to that primary source.
- The shop is not in good standing for violating Etsy policies.

Sellers are responsible for refunding costs for orders outside the eligibility requirements.

Buyers are responsible for customs duties, import duties, or other government charges payable on delivery. Items late or not received because of buyer delay or refusal to pay those charges do not qualify. Sellers are not responsible for refunds in that situation, but may offer them at their discretion.

## How do I qualify?

Etsy Purchase Protection automatically covers qualifying orders with no setup required.

The process begins after:

- A buyer reaches out through a Help request.
- The buyer opens a case.

Orders are eligible if:

- They were shipped within the stated processing time.
- Valid tracking was provided or the order was mailed with an Etsy shipping label.
- The listing has an estimated delivery date.
- The received item matches the listing photos and description.
- The seller meets Etsy customer service standards.
- The seller responded to the buyer’s Help with Order message within 48 hours.
- It is the first damaged-item instance in the calendar year.
- Duties and import fees are included upfront for orders shipping into the US.

If these requirements are met, Etsy covers up to $250 of the refund amount. Sellers are responsible for costs for orders that do not qualify.

Sellers must ship on time and provide tracking when available. Etsy may use carrier tracking scans and uploaded tracking to confirm timely shipping.

## How it works

If a seller has concerns about a closed case for an item that arrived damaged or different than described, the seller can contact Etsy to dispute the outcome.

A trained specialist reviews the case and outcome. A final decision is sent by email within 1-3 business days.

For an item never received or arriving 7+ days after the estimated delivery window, Etsy may already have all required information. If a case is still open for an item never received, the seller should use Shop Manager to communicate with the buyer in the case log.
