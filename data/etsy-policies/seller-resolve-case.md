# How to Resolve a Case from a Buyer

Cases indicate that a buyer has an unresolved issue with an order. A case does not necessarily count against a shop when the seller meets Etsy’s customer service standard.

If a buyer’s issue qualifies for Etsy Purchase Protection, encourage the buyer to open a case for Etsy to review. Etsy uses shipping status and Purchase Protection eligibility criteria for most case decisions. If more information is needed, Etsy notifies the seller in the case log and by email.

For cases opened before May 7, 2026, the Purchase Protection policy in effect at that time applies.

## What can a buyer open a case for?

- Non-delivery: the order did not arrive.
- Not as described: the received item was not what the listing described.
- Item arrived damaged.
- Item arrived 7+ days after its maximum estimated delivery date window.

## When can a buyer open a case?

The order’s maximum estimated delivery date must have passed and at least 48 hours must have passed after the buyer contacted the seller. For a non-delivery case, the buyer must wait 7+ days after the maximum estimated delivery date has passed. Once eligible, buyers have 30 days to open a case.

## How to resolve a case

Etsy reviews the order and makes a resolution. If the order is eligible for Purchase Protection, the buyer receives a full refund. Etsy covers up to $250, or the converted equivalent in the seller’s local currency, and any remaining amount is charged to the seller. The seller does not need to issue the refund themselves.

If Etsy requests more information about an open case, the seller must respond. Once the case is closed, the seller and buyer receive an email with the resolution.

Etsy Purchase Protection is not an insurance policy, warranty, or guarantee. Etsy decides in its sole discretion whether a transaction receives protection. Etsy may resolve the case on the seller’s behalf, including issuing a refund and recouping funds from the Payment account.

## How do I work with my buyer to resolve the case?

If the case is open, work with the buyer through Cases in Shop Manager.

1. Sign in to Etsy.com and go to Shop Manager.
2. Go to Help and then Cases.
3. Go to the Cases reported about your shop tab and select the applicable case ID.
4. Use Add Your Comment to communicate while the case is active. Depending on the order, you may also issue a refund or update shipping information.

Keep all case comments and information in the case log. Upload screenshots or photos directly to the case page when needed.

If the buyer and seller cannot resolve the problem through Messages, the buyer may open a case. A case is a formal report, and the seller must work with the buyer to resolve it.

## Case-specific help

For a non-delivery case, provide proof that the item was shipped to the address on the Etsy receipt or the address agreed in Etsy Messages. Provide a valid tracking number.

For a not-as-described or damaged-in-mail case, comment in the case log, acknowledge the customer’s concern, and focus on resolving the issue. A case agent reviews the case and makes a determination.

## When a case is closed

Cases usually close automatically once the issue is resolved. They may close automatically when:

- The seller issues a full refund through Etsy Payments.
- A non-delivery case has no tracking information or tracking shows delivery to the receipt address.
- The buyer closes the case after being satisfied with the resolution.

If Etsy requests more information about an open case, the seller must respond. If the buyer paid through personal PayPal instead of Etsy Payments, Etsy may require proof of a PayPal refund before closing the case.
