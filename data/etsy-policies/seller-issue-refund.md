# How to Issue a Full or Partial Refund For an Order

You can issue a full or partial refund for an order in your Shop Manager.

## To refund an order

1. On Etsy.com, open Shop Manager.
2. Select Orders.
3. Choose the three-dot icon next to the order you want to refund.
4. Choose Refund.
5. Select a reason for issuing a refund and add an optional message to the buyer.
6. To refund the entire transaction, select the box by Issue a full refund. To partially refund the transaction, enter the amount to refund next to the item.
7. Select Review refund.
8. Select Submit.

Canceling is separate from refunding. Cancel the order if needed.

## What can I issue a refund for?

You can issue a refund in accordance with your shop policies. You can also issue refunds if:

- You agree to issue a refund to the buyer.
- The buyer is overcharged for shipping.
- The buyer forgot to apply a shop coupon.

## How do I pay for a refund?

If you use Etsy Payments, the refund funds are deducted from your Payment account. If the Payment account does not have enough funds to cover the refund, the credit card on file is charged for the remaining amount.

If you use another payment method, refund the order through that payment method.

## Will Etsy refund my seller fees?

If you refund a buyer through Shop Manager, Etsy transaction and processing fees for the refunded transactions are automatically refunded to your Payment account. If you also canceled the transaction, listing fees are automatically credited to the account.

## How will the buyer receive the refund?

When you issue a refund, Etsy may give the buyer the option to receive the refund in Etsy credit or back to the original payment method. The seller cannot choose or modify the buyer’s refund method.

If a buyer asks about the status of a refund after it has been issued, direct them to Etsy Support.

## When can I issue a refund?

If you use Etsy Payments, you can issue a refund after the payment is processed and before 180 days after the payment is processed. After 180 days, you cannot refund an order through Etsy Payments.

If the shop policy allows refunds after 180 days, refund the buyer through a payment method outside Etsy, such as PayPal. Etsy will not be able to refund seller fees for this type of refund.

## When can’t I issue a refund?

If the Issue a Refund button is unavailable, the order is non-refundable. This may happen if 180 days have passed since the purchase date or if the buyer has filed a chargeback. The buyer must work with the financial institution that handled the chargeback.
