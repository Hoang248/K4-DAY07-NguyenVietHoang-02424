# What is an Estimated Delivery Date?

## Quick answer

To open a case, the estimated delivery date for the order must have passed and at least 48 hours must have passed since the buyer sent the seller a Help with order request.

- Non-delivery: 7+ days must have passed since the maximum estimated delivery date.
- Not as described or arrived damaged: the maximum estimated delivery date must have passed.

The estimated delivery date provided at checkout is available on the Help with your order page.

## How is the estimated delivery date calculated?

The estimated delivery date is based on the processing time for the items and the transit time for the shipping carrier.

Processing time + carrier transit time = estimated delivery date.

For example, if processing takes 3-5 days and carrier transit takes 2-4 days, the estimated delivery date is 5-9 days from today.

If the seller completes the order earlier than the expected ship date, the estimated delivery date is recalculated.

A non-delivery case can be opened only 7+ days after the maximum estimated delivery date provided at checkout has passed.

## How do I see the estimated delivery date before purchasing?

On Etsy.com:

1. Select an item.
2. Scroll to Shipping and return policies.
3. Find Order today to get by with a date range.

On the Etsy app:

1. Select an item.
2. Scroll to Shipping & policies.
3. Find Est. Delivery or Estimated delivery with a date range.

The range considers the seller’s processing time and the postal carrier’s latest transit times.

## How do I see my order’s estimated delivery date?

On Etsy.com:

1. Sign in and select the Your Account icon.
2. Select Purchases.
3. If available, the estimated delivery date appears with the order.

On the Etsy app:

1. Tap You.
2. Tap Purchases.
3. If available, the estimated delivery date appears next to the item image.

Not all orders have estimated delivery dates. They depend on the order’s shipping settings, which are set by the seller.
