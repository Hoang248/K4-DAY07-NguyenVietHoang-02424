# How to Open a Case

## Quick answer

If the order's maximum estimated delivery date has passed and you contacted the seller more than 48 hours ago, you can select Open a case.

You can open a case after you’ve contacted the seller and your order becomes eligible. For qualifying orders, you’ll receive a refund for an item that doesn’t arrive, arrives damaged, or doesn’t match the item description or photos.

## How to open a case

1. Sign in to Etsy.com and go to Your account. On the Etsy app, sign in and tap You.
2. Go to Purchases and reviews.
3. Select Help with order next to the order. On the Etsy app, tap the order and then tap Help with order.
4. Select the reason you need help.
5. Select how you prefer to receive your refund, if one is issued. The options vary based on currency settings, location, and order eligibility. The selection cannot be changed later.
6. In the Let us know what went wrong box, write supporting details about your case.
7. Select Submit to Etsy.

To open a case, you must have an Etsy account. If you ordered as a guest, create an Etsy account and link your order.

## Case types

- Non-delivery: you paid for an order but did not receive it, or received it 7+ days after the estimated delivery date window provided at checkout.
- Not as described: the color, design, model, quantity, or condition differs from the listing description and photos.
- Item arrived damaged: you received the item but it was damaged.

## When can I open a case?

For physical items and digital items ready for immediate download:

- The estimated delivery date provided at purchase has passed.
- At least 48 hours have passed since you sent the seller a Help with order request.

For digital items made to order:

- You downloaded the item, or 7 days have passed since purchase.
- At least 48 hours have passed since you sent the seller a Help with order request.

The case prompt shows when the case can start and how long you have to open it. Once eligible, the order is no longer eligible 30 days from its estimated delivery date.

## What should I say to the seller?

Explain why you are contacting the seller and your desired resolution. Include order details and photos if the item is damaged or different from what you ordered.

## How Etsy will help

Etsy works with the buyer and seller to collect information and reach a resolution. For qualifying orders, Purchase Protection provides a full refund when the item is damaged, not delivered, not as described, or arrives 7+ days after the maximum estimated delivery date.

After opening a case, communication with the agent takes place in the case log. Provide requested information such as photographs, measurements, third-party evaluations, and anything else needed.

Items arriving late due to forces outside the seller’s control, such as a carrier strike, severe weather, war, or civil unrest, do not qualify for Purchase Protection.

## How to close a case

1. On Etsy.com, go to Your account.
2. Go to Purchases and reviews.
3. Select View case next to the order.
4. Choose the case.
5. Select Close.

A case closes automatically when a full refund is issued or when the buyer contacts a card provider or PayPal to dispute the charge.

## How refunds work

If Etsy determines that an item does not match the listing, the seller must refund the item cost, original shipping cost, and return shipping cost. In some cases Etsy may issue the refund for the seller.

If Etsy credit is selected, the refund appears in the Gift cards & Etsy Credits balance once approved. Refunds processed by Etsy must take place within 180 days of the transaction. After 180 days, Etsy cannot support the refund.

If payment used standalone PayPal, the buyer may need to open a PayPal dispute within 180 days of purchase.

If the buyer filed a chargeback, the seller cannot issue a refund on Etsy and the buyer cannot open a case; the buyer must contact the financial institution.
