# Etsy Purchase Protection Program

## Qualifying order issues

Etsy’s Purchase Protection program provides a full refund for qualifying orders when an item:

- Doesn’t arrive.
- Arrives damaged.
- Arrives 7+ days after the maximum estimated delivery date window provided at checkout.
- Differs significantly from the item description or photos, such as the wrong material or color.

## If an order does not qualify

If an order does not meet the criteria for Etsy Purchase Protection, check the seller’s policies for cancellations, returns, or exchanges.

Buyers are responsible for customs duties, import duties, or other charges imposed by their country's government, payable on delivery. Items that arrive late or are not received because of buyer delay or refusal to pay charges owed on delivery do not qualify.

## How do I get my refund?

The first step is to reach out to the seller by starting a Help request.

If the issue has not been resolved and it has been 48 hours since you first messaged the seller, you may be able to ask Etsy to step in and help you get a refund. Eligibility depends on the case requirements.

Once refund eligibility is confirmed, Etsy sends a confirmation email.

Items that arrive late due to forces outside the seller’s control, such as a carrier strike, severe weather, war, civil unrest, or similar event, do not qualify.

## I purchased as a guest

You must have an Etsy account to start a Help request. If you purchased as a guest, connect the order to a new or existing Etsy account to be eligible for the Purchase Protection program.

## What happens to the seller if I request a refund?

An occasional case opened against a seller will not negatively impact the seller or shop. Etsy reviews cases carefully to ensure the right decision is made.
